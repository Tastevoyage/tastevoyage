🇹🇷 Taste of Turkey

Discover the rich flavors, ancient traditions and diverse culinary culture of Turkey.

The Heart of Turkish Cuisine

Turkish cuisine brings together flavors and traditions from Anatolia, the Mediterranean, the Middle East and neighboring regions.

Fresh vegetables, grains, yogurt, herbs, olive oil, meats and spices are important parts of Turkish cooking.

From traditional breakfasts and meze to kebabs and famous desserts, Turkish cuisine offers an incredible variety of dishes and regional specialties.

Famous Turkish Dishes

Turkish cuisine is famous for its generous variety of savory dishes, breads, pastries and desserts.

🥙 Kebab

Grilled or roasted meat prepared in many regional styles and served with bread, vegetables and sauces.

🥐 Börek

Thin pastry filled with cheese, vegetables, meat or other savory ingredients.

🍮 Baklava

Layers of delicate pastry filled with nuts and finished with sweet syrup or honey.

Essential Turkish Ingredients

Turkish cooking combines fresh produce, grains, herbs, dairy products and aromatic spices.

🫒 Olive Oil

Especially important in Mediterranean and Aegean Turkish cooking.

🥛 Yogurt

Used in sauces, soups, side dishes and many traditional meals.

🌶️ Spices

Paprika, cumin, mint and other spices add depth and aroma to Turkish dishes.

🌿 Fresh Herbs

Parsley, mint, dill and other herbs bring freshness to many dishes.

Regional Turkish Cuisine

Turkey's location between Europe and Asia has helped create an exceptionally diverse culinary landscape.

🌊 Aegean Region

Known for olive oil, vegetables, herbs and Mediterranean-style dishes.

🏙️ Istanbul

A crossroads of culinary traditions with influences from many parts of the country and beyond.

🔥 Southeastern Turkey

Famous for kebabs, spicy flavors, pistachios and rich traditional dishes.

🌿 Black Sea Region

Known for corn, tea, seafood, greens and distinctive regional recipes.

Turkish Food Culture

Food is deeply connected with hospitality, family and social life in Turkish culture.

☕ Turkish Tea

Tea is an important part of everyday social life and hospitality.

🍽️ Meze

Small dishes are often shared at the table, allowing diners to enjoy many different flavors.

🍳 Turkish Breakfast

Traditional breakfast can include breads, cheeses, olives, eggs, vegetables and other foods.

A Taste of Turkish Food History

Turkish cuisine has developed through centuries of cultural exchange across Anatolia and the wider region.

The cuisines of the Ottoman Empire brought together culinary traditions from many different peoples and geographical areas.

Ingredients such as wheat, lamb, yogurt, olive oil, vegetables, dried fruits and spices became important parts of the culinary tradition.

Regional cooking continued to develop according to local agriculture, climate and cultural traditions.

Today, Turkish cuisine remains closely connected with hospitality, family gatherings and centuries-old cooking traditions.
