Thai Green Curry

A fragrant Thai classic filled with herbs, coconut milk, vegetables and layers of aromatic flavor.

Preparation 20 minutes
Cooking 25 minutes
Servings 4 people
Cuisine Thai

About Thai Green Curry

Thai Green Curry is one of Thailand's best-known dishes. Its distinctive green color comes from fresh green chilies and aromatic herbs used in the curry paste.

Coconut milk gives the curry a smooth and creamy texture, while ingredients such as lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime and Thai basil create its characteristic fragrance.

It can be prepared with chicken, seafood, beef, tofu or vegetables, making it a versatile dish for different tastes.

Ingredients

  • 400 ml coconut milk
  • 2–3 tablespoons green curry paste
  • 400 g chicken or tofu
  • 1 small eggplant
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 cup Thai basil leaves
  • 2 kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 stalk lemongrass
  • 1 teaspoon fish sauce
  • 1 teaspoon palm sugar
  • Green chilies
  • Cooking oil

How To Prepare

01. Prepare the ingredients

Slice the chicken or tofu and prepare the vegetables. Bruise the lemongrass and tear the kaffir lime leaves slightly to release their aroma.

02. Fry the curry paste

Heat a little oil in a pan. Add the green curry paste and cook gently until its fragrance becomes strong.

03. Add coconut milk

Add part of the coconut milk and stir well with the curry paste. Allow it to simmer gently.

04. Cook the main ingredients

Add the chicken or tofu and vegetables. Add the remaining coconut milk and simmer until everything is cooked through.

05. Balance the flavors

Add fish sauce and palm sugar according to taste. Finish with Thai basil and fresh green chilies.

06. Serve

Serve the curry hot with steamed jasmine rice. Garnish with fresh basil or sliced chili.

The Story Behind Thai Green Curry

Thai curry traditions developed through the use of local herbs, chilies, spices and coconut. Green curry became especially recognizable because of its use of fresh green ingredients.

Thai cuisine is famous for balancing several flavors in one dish. Sweet, salty, spicy, sour and aromatic elements can work together rather than one flavor dominating the entire meal.

Today, Thai Green Curry is enjoyed both inside Thailand and around the world, with many regional and personal variations.

Chef's Tips

  • Use fresh herbs whenever possible for a stronger fragrance.
  • Add the coconut milk gradually rather than boiling everything aggressively.
  • Adjust the amount of green curry paste according to your preferred level of heat.
  • Thai basil added near the end keeps its aroma fresh.
  • Serve immediately with warm jasmine rice for the best texture and flavor.
