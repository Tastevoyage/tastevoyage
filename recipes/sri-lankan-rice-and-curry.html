Sri Lankan Rice & Curry

A traditional journey through the rich, aromatic and colorful flavors of Sri Lanka.

Preparation 20 minutes
Cooking 45 minutes
Servings 4 people
Cuisine Sri Lankan

About This Dish

Sri Lankan Rice & Curry is more than just a meal. It is one of the most recognizable expressions of Sri Lankan food culture. A typical plate can bring together steamed rice, several vegetable curries, dhal, sambols, mallung and sometimes fish, chicken or meat.

The beauty of Sri Lankan rice and curry comes from the balance between different flavors and textures. Coconut milk creates richness, roasted spices add warmth, fresh herbs bring aroma, while sambols and pickles provide brightness and heat.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups white rice
  • 1 cup red lentils
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 2 teaspoons curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon chili powder
  • 1 cup coconut milk
  • Green chilies
  • Curry leaves
  • Pandan leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Cooking oil

How To Prepare

01. Prepare the rice

Wash the rice thoroughly and cook it with the appropriate amount of water until the grains become soft and fluffy.

02. Prepare the dhal curry

Wash the lentils and cook them with turmeric, onion, garlic and salt. Add coconut milk and simmer gently until creamy.

03. Prepare the curry base

Heat oil and add onion, curry leaves, pandan leaves and green chilies. Add the spices and cook gently until aromatic.

04. Add the vegetables

Add your chosen vegetables and cook with coconut milk and spices until tender.

05. Serve

Place steamed rice on a plate and arrange the different curries around it. Finish with a fresh sambol or mallung for extra flavor.

The Story Behind Sri Lankan Rice & Curry

Rice has been an important part of Sri Lankan food culture for centuries. The island's agricultural traditions, especially its ancient rice-growing communities, helped make rice a central part of everyday meals.

Over generations, Sri Lankan cooks developed a remarkable variety of curries using local ingredients such as coconut, cinnamon, curry leaves, pandan, chili and traditional spice blends.

Unlike a single curry served with rice, a Sri Lankan "rice and curry" meal often contains several dishes at once. This variety is one of the characteristics that makes the meal so memorable.
