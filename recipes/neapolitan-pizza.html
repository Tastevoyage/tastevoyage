Neapolitan Pizza

A simple Italian classic built around dough, tomatoes, mozzarella and the magic of a hot oven.

Preparation 30 minutes
Resting 8–24 hours
Servings 2 pizzas
Cuisine Italian

About Neapolitan Pizza

Neapolitan pizza is one of Italy's most famous culinary traditions. It is known for its soft, airy crust, simple tomato topping and fresh mozzarella.

Rather than using a large number of toppings, the traditional style focuses on a small number of quality ingredients. The dough, tomatoes, cheese, olive oil and basil work together to create a balanced flavor.

Ingredients

  • 500 g Italian-style flour
  • 325 ml water
  • 15 g salt
  • 1–2 g dry yeast
  • San Marzano-style tomatoes
  • Fresh mozzarella
  • Fresh basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil

How To Prepare

01. Make the dough

Mix the water and salt, then gradually add the flour and yeast. Knead until the dough becomes smooth and elastic.

02. Let the dough rest

Cover the dough and allow it to ferment slowly. A long fermentation develops better flavor and texture.

03. Shape the pizza

Divide the dough into portions and gently stretch each one into a round pizza base. Keep the edge slightly thicker than the center.

04. Add the toppings

Spread a thin layer of crushed tomatoes over the dough. Add mozzarella, fresh basil and a small drizzle of olive oil.

05. Bake at very high heat

Bake the pizza in a very hot oven until the crust is puffed and lightly charred and the cheese is melted.

The Story Behind Neapolitan Pizza

Neapolitan pizza is closely associated with Naples in southern Italy, where pizza developed as an affordable and popular food for local communities.

The combination of a simple dough, tomato, mozzarella, basil and olive oil became one of the most recognizable expressions of Italian cuisine.

Today, Neapolitan-style pizza is enjoyed around the world, while traditional pizza makers continue to focus on fermentation, high-temperature baking and carefully selected ingredients.
