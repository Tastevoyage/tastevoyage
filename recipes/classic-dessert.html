Classic Dessert

A creamy, sweet and comforting dessert made for special moments and everyday cravings.

Preparation 15 minutes
Cooking 25 minutes
Servings 4 people
Category Dessert

About This Dessert

Desserts are found in almost every food culture around the world. They can be simple or elaborate, warm or chilled, creamy or crunchy.

This classic dessert combines creamy texture with a gentle sweetness and a touch of vanilla. It is easy to prepare and can be served with fresh fruit, nuts or a little chocolate.

Ingredients

  • 500 ml milk
  • 200 ml fresh cream
  • 3 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons cornflour
  • 2 tablespoons cold water
  • Fresh fruits
  • Chopped nuts
  • Chocolate shavings

How To Prepare

01. Prepare the mixture

Add milk, cream and sugar to a saucepan. Stir gently over medium heat.

02. Add the thickener

Mix cornflour with cold water until smooth. Slowly add it to the warm milk mixture while stirring continuously.

03. Cook until creamy

Continue stirring over low to medium heat until the mixture becomes smooth and slightly thick.

04. Add vanilla

Remove from the heat and stir in vanilla extract.

05. Chill

Pour into serving glasses or bowls. Allow to cool, then refrigerate until completely chilled.

06. Garnish and serve

Add fresh fruit, chopped nuts or chocolate shavings before serving.

The Story Behind Desserts

Sweet dishes have been part of human food traditions for centuries. Different cultures developed desserts using ingredients available in their local regions.

Milk, fruits, honey, sugar, grains, nuts and spices have all played important roles in the development of desserts around the world.

Today, desserts continue to evolve as cooks combine traditional techniques with modern ingredients and presentation styles.

Serving Tips

  • Chill the dessert thoroughly before serving.
  • Add fresh fruit just before serving for a refreshing contrast.
  • Toasted nuts can add a delicious crunchy texture.
  • A little dark chocolate can balance the sweetness.
  • Serve in transparent glasses for a more attractive presentation.
